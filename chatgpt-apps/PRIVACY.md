# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026. Replace "the operator" below with your legal/business name before submitting — the developer name on the ChatGPT submission form must match it exactly._

These ChatGPT apps — **QR Code Studio**, **Calendar Invite & Date Math**, **Timesheet & Overtime Calculator**, **Mortgage & Loan Calculator** and **Verified Citations** — are operated by the operator.

## What we process

Each app receives only the information ChatGPT sends to the specific tool you invoke (for example the text to encode in a QR code, the event details for a calendar invite, the hours on a timesheet, loan figures, or the reference you want cited). We use that information solely to compute the result you asked for and return it to ChatGPT.

## What we store

Nothing. The apps are stateless: no database, no user accounts, no cookies. Download links contain the data needed to rebuild the file you asked for, signed so they cannot be altered, and expire after 7 days. We do not keep copies of generated files.

## Logs

Our servers write operational logs containing the app name, tool name, timestamp, duration and success/failure of each call. Logs do not contain the content of your requests. Logs are retained for up to 30 days for reliability monitoring.

## Third parties

- **Verified Citations** sends the title, DOI or reference text you provide to the public [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/privacy/) and [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) APIs to look up bibliographic records. No other app contacts any external service.
- **Calendar Invite & Date Math** offers links that open Google Calendar or Outlook with the event pre-filled. Those pages are operated by Google and Microsoft under their own privacy policies; nothing is sent to them until you click.

## Children

The apps are suitable for users aged 13 and over and do not knowingly collect information from children.
